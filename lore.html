Hi! I’m glad to see you here. I started designing when I got bored of playing The Sims 2 and discovered Photoshop on the family computer in 2007 & I haven’t looked back since.

I enjoy problem solving, learning new things and bringing my ideas to life - so I’ve tried my hand at many areas of design. But what I love the most is having fun with the visuals!

Anna Volkova is a designer working across interface, systems, and visual design to create products that are rigorous underneath, intuitive in use, and alive with personality.

I’ve lived in Utrecht, NL, Antwerp, BE & Yekaterinburg, RU
