Overview

Most file tools, and most AI assistants, send your files to a data center. On My Disk doesn’t — the files and the assistant both live on your device.

The design problem was making that believable: privacy is invisible by default, so the interface had to show people where their data was at every point.

How do you make users trust that their files never leave their device?
