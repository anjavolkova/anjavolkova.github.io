Designing a file-sharing tool that keeps your data private

role
Product designer
team
1 engineer, 1 designer
skills
Product, UX
timeline
2024–2025
client
Bineon

Most file tools, and most AI assistants, send your files to a data center. On My Disk doesn’t — files and the assistant both live on your device. The design problem was making that believable: privacy is invisible by default, so the interface had to show people where their data was at every point.
